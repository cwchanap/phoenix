# Phoenix placeholder audio

Most WAV files here are project-generated placeholder tones created for the HPA-599 MVP closeout. They contain no externally sourced recording or composition and require no third-party attribution. They are intentionally disposable if authored audio replaces them later. The two HPA-440 ambience files are different: `river-ambience.wav` and `rain-ambience.wav` were generated by ElevenLabs (see their section below) under the repository owner's paid subscription, whose license terms permit commercial use — including distribution inside the exported `Phoenix.zip` — without attribution.

## HPA-459 farming success cues

`farm-hoe.wav`, `farm-plant.wav`, `farm-water.wav`, and `farm-harvest.wav` are project-generated with the same throwaway Python stdlib recipe as the HPA-599 tones (`wave`/`math`/`random` only; mono 16-bit 22050 Hz, <0.5 s each). No synthesis script is committed; regenerate by re-running the recipe below and normalizing peak amplitude to 0.85.

| File | Length | Timbre | Recipe |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `farm-hoe.wav` | 0.22 s | low dirt thud | 85 Hz sine, 45 ms decay, plus a 12 ms white-noise burst |
| `farm-plant.wav` | 0.18 s | droplet pluck | single sine sweeping 620→170 Hz (exp. pitch falloff τ=30 ms) with 50 ms decay |
| `farm-water.wav` | 0.35 s | noise swish | one-sample high-passed white noise under a single full-length sine-squared swell |
| `farm-harvest.wav` | 0.28 s | bright two-note pop | 780 Hz blip then a 1170 Hz blip entering at 60 ms (60 ms decay), plus 20 ms sparkle noise |

All four map one-to-one through `GameHud._sfx_for_code()` to the farming success codes (`SOIL_TILLED`, `CROP_PLANTED`, `CROP_WATERED`, `CROP_HARVESTED`) and play through the existing SFX player, so the Sound setting governs them automatically.

## HPA-440 river and rain ambience

`river-ambience.wav` is a 5-second loop of nearby moving water for the west river, and `rain-ambience.wav` is a 5-second loop of steady rain for weather playback. Both are mono PCM-16 at 22050 Hz, exactly 5.0 s / 110250 frames.

Provenance (per the repo audio convention, see CLAUDE.md): generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`) through the ElevenLabs MCP creative flow `ATS3d0Ki7wCgYSmjD2rL`, parameters `duration_seconds=5.0`, `loop=true`, `prompt_influence=0.7`; four takes per asset, selected by measurement — river take `jFveZfGFwAWKVz1nu7qo`, rain take `jo08JStJ0FWzI5fcrnpA`. Prompts (verbatim): river — "Gentle river water flowing over smooth stones. Steady low and mid frequency burbling water bed, soft rolling texture, continuous and even, no splashes, no birds, no wind, no dramatic events, no silence gaps. Calm ambient background loop."; rain — "Steady soft rainfall on a quiet homestead. Dense gentle broadband rain bed with warm low-mid body, continuous and even, no thunder, no wind gusts, no individual water drops, no dramatic events, no silence gaps. Calm ambient background loop."

Post-processing (disposable tooling; recipe recorded here) ran in two passes:

Pass 1 applied to the 44.1 kHz stereo masters: downmix to 22050 Hz mono PCM-16; strip leading/trailing near-silence (the selected takes carried bed-level signal to their edges, so the strip removed nothing); river got a one-pole lowpass tilt at 1400 Hz to pull energy toward the low/mid water bed; rain got soft compression of sparse spectral peaks (knee 0.18 FS); each loop was rebuilt with a 100 ms equal-power circular crossfade — a 110250-frame body beginning 100 ms into the source take (which ran slightly longer than the requested 5.0 s), its tail crossfaded into its head so the wrap replays adjacent source samples and the output length is exactly 110250 frames (5.0 s); finally each bed was scaled to a target mix RMS (river 0.0557, rain 0.0697).

Pass 2 reworked the committed buffers after review (no regeneration — the same takes): (a) periodic-comb repair — bins at 200 Hz multiples exceeding 3x the local ±5–9-bin median floor are capped to the floor with phase preserved, removing the take's baked-in periodic lines (rain carried a ~200 Hz comb up to ~5000x local floor — a faint pitched hum; river a weaker version); (b) zero-phase spectral shaping by circular convolution, preserving the wrap by construction — river re-darkened so 90–900 Hz dominates (unity ≤750 Hz, −6 dB @1300 Hz, −10 @2200, −16 @4000, −20 @11025; ≤90 Hz suppressed) and rain band-limited so 700–7000 Hz dominates (+6 dB @400 Hz and +4 @700 for low-mid body, −3 @7000, −9 @9000, −12 @11025, plus residual DC removed); (c) wrap-centered gain repair — a smooth circular bump (≤ ~+4 dB across the last ~350 ms and first ~150 ms) removes the ~3.5 dB once-per-loop trough the ±32-sample seam check could not see; (d) both beds scaled to an A-weighted level ~4 dB below the imported music bed (−44.7 dBFS(A)), so they measure strictly below `farm-day-loop.wav` under both unweighted and A-weighted RMS; (e) requantized to mono PCM-16 at exactly 110250 frames. Committed source peaks: river 0.057, rain 0.055 — both under the 0.30 guard. MCP generation is not byte-reproducible; the committed WAVs are the artifacts of record and rerunning a generation produces new takes, not these files.

Both sidecars import as full-clip Forward loops (`edit/loop_mode=2`, `edit/loop_begin=0`, `edit/loop_end=110250`) at the repository-default `compress/mode=2` (QOA); the PCM fallback was not needed. `loop_end` is the exclusive wrap point — Godot replays `[loop_begin, loop_end)`, so the full clip requires the frame count; the earlier `-1` resolved to `frames-1` and silently dropped the last sample every pass.

Acceptance verdict (measurement-based imported-stream review — headless quantitative analysis of the QOA-decoded imported `AudioStreamWAV` samples, not a human listening session): each bed was analyzed as a six-period (≥ 30 s) concatenation of its imported buffer. All five loop-wrap transitions measure |Δ| 0.0029 (river) / 0.0066 (rain) — the 67.9th / 53.8th percentile of the concatenated signal's ordinary adjacent-sample deltas — and the largest delta within ±32 samples of any wrap (0.0128 river / 0.0593 rain) stays below the global delta maximum (0.0193 / 0.0687, occurring elsewhere in ordinary playback), so the imported streams loop without a seam-localized click or level jump and `compress/mode=2` needed no fallback. The once-per-loop 0.2 Hz component of the 100 ms RMS envelope measures 3.1% (river) / 3.7% (rain) of the mean envelope — below the 10% gate and below the strongest ≤2 Hz component (5.4% @1.4 Hz / 6.1% @0.4 Hz); aggregate ≤2 Hz modulation 10.5% / 11.1%, within-period envelope max/min 2.00 / 1.83, and the wrap-region level is now flat (last-200 ms envelope −0.39 / −0.14 dB vs mean, first-200 ms −0.53 / +0.49 dB — no once-per-loop dip). River energy is 64.4% in 90–900 Hz with 34.9% as 900–2200 Hz texture and 0.7% above (centroid ≈ 855 Hz); rain is 85.8% in 700–7000 Hz with 4.1% low-mid body below 700 Hz and 10.1% air above 7 kHz (centroid ≈ 3.6 kHz); the periodic-comb lines were repaired, and the beds remain clearly distinguishable by spectrum. Equal-gain mix vs the imported `farm-day-loop.wav` (−19.7 dBFS / −40.7 dBFS(A)): the subordination gate is decided by imported-stream RMS under both unweighted and A-weighted measurement, each strictly below the music bed (< 0 dB relative) — river −38.9 dBFS / rain −40.0 dBFS unweighted (−19.2 / −20.3 dB) and −44.7 / −44.7 dBFS(A) (−4.0 dB) both pass. Against the farming one-shots the beds are also less prominent under both metrics: `farm-hoe.wav` −16.1 dBFS / −35.0 dBFS(A) and `farm-harvest.wav` −13.4 / −18.5 put the beds ≥ 22.8 dB below unweighted and ≥ 9.7 dB below A-weighted; imported crest ratios are 4.9x river / 5.0x rain vs 5.6x `farm-hoe.wav` / 4.0x `farm-harvest.wav`. Residual risk: measurement is not listening — the merge-time acceptance is the measurement review above; the human listening gate was removed at owner direction.
