# Phoenix placeholder audio

These WAV files are project-generated placeholder tones created for the HPA-599 MVP closeout. They contain no externally sourced recording or composition and require no third-party attribution. They are intentionally disposable if authored audio replaces them later.

## HPA-459 farming success cues

`farm-hoe.wav`, `farm-plant.wav`, `farm-water.wav`, and `farm-harvest.wav` are project-generated with the same throwaway Python stdlib recipe as the HPA-599 tones (`wave`/`math`/`random` only; mono 16-bit 22050 Hz, <0.5 s each). No synthesis script is committed; regenerate by re-running the recipe below and normalizing peak amplitude to 0.85.

| File | Length | Timbre | Recipe |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `farm-hoe.wav` | 0.22 s | low dirt thud | 85 Hz sine, 45 ms decay, plus a 12 ms white-noise burst |
| `farm-plant.wav` | 0.18 s | droplet pluck | single sine sweeping 620→170 Hz (exp. pitch falloff τ=30 ms) with 50 ms decay |
| `farm-water.wav` | 0.35 s | noise swish | one-sample high-passed white noise under a single full-length sine-squared swell |
| `farm-harvest.wav` | 0.28 s | bright two-note pop | 780 Hz blip then a 1170 Hz blip entering at 60 ms (60 ms decay), plus 20 ms sparkle noise |

All four map one-to-one through `GameHud._sfx_for_code()` to the farming success codes (`SOIL_TILLED`, `CROP_PLANTED`, `CROP_WATERED`, `CROP_HARVESTED`) and play through the existing SFX player, so the Sound setting governs them automatically.

## HPA-440 river and rain ambience

`river-ambience.wav` is a 5-second loop of nearby moving water for the west river, and `rain-ambience.wav` is a 5-second loop of steady rain for weather playback. Both are mono PCM-16 at 22050 Hz, exactly 5.0 s / 110250 frames, generated by `tools/generate_hpa_440_ambience.py` from fixed seeds (`44001` river, `44002` rain) with no external samples and no third-party attribution. Peak/RMS of the committed sources: river 0.246 / 0.0557 (-12.2 / -25.1 dBFS), rain 0.187 / 0.0697 (-14.6 / -23.1 dBFS). The generator is the recipe source of truth; rerunning it in the same environment reproduces both WAVs byte-for-byte.

Both sidecars import as full-clip Forward loops (`edit/loop_mode=2`, `edit/loop_begin=0`, `edit/loop_end=-1`) at the repository-default `compress/mode=2` (QOA); the PCM fallback was not needed.

Acceptance verdict (measurement-based imported-stream review — headless quantitative analysis of the imported `AudioStreamWAV` samples, not a human listening session): the imported streams loop without a seam — the last-to-first-sample transition sits at the 77th (river) / 78th (rain) percentile of the file's ordinary adjacent-sample deltas with the seam-window maximum equal to the global maximum, so no click or level jump exists at the loop point; the buffer is one periodic 5-second period, so a 30-second playback is five identical periods with only the designed slow amplitude breathing (≈ ±13–17%, 0.4/1.2 Hz river, 1.0/2.2 Hz rain) and no distinct five-second pulse. River energy is 66% in 90–900 Hz with light texture to ~2200 Hz (spectral centroid ≈ 1.3 kHz, no tonal spikes), rain energy is dominant across ≈ 700–7000 Hz with a restrained low-mid body (centroid ≈ 4.6 kHz, no thunder-like transients). At equal player gain both beds sit 3–5 dB below `farm-day-loop.wav` RMS (river -25.0, rain -23.0 vs music -19.7 dBFS imported) with no transients, and both are far quieter and lower-crest than `farm-hoe.wav` / `farm-harvest.wav` (0.85 peaks, 6–9 dB hotter RMS), so ambience stays subordinate to music and farming feedback. River and Rain are clearly distinguishable by spectrum (centroids 1.3 kHz vs 4.6 kHz).

HPA-402 is the runtime consumer: it owns players, path wiring, Sound-setting integration, the -12 dB ambience headroom, weather-driven rain start/stop, and lifecycle. The forward-loop import property is already proven here and needs no re-verification downstream.
