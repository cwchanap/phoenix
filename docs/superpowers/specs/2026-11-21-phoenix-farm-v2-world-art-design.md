## Outcome and visual source of truth

Expand the authored farm from 24×20 to **48×40 logical cells** at the existing 64×32 isometric grid. Preserve the short daily gameplay loop while replacing the raised, visibly repeating terrain and grass-island props with the continuous world shown in the approved Linear mockup.

Treat the following HPA-863 attachments as the approved design inputs, **not runtime data**:
- farm-map-v2-plan.png: numbered 48×40 top-down arrangement.
- farm-map-v2-layout.json: terrain regions, 60 object records, fence positions, footprints, zone names and blocking/decorative intent.
- farm-map-v2-blockout-*.png: expected farm size and front-door, field and storehouse-yard camera compositions.
- world-mockup-vs-game.jpg: target grass, paths, building grounding, soil, crop and HUD composition.

HPA-861 has committed new cutouts, candidate terrain, soil, crops and review scenes under `assets/sprites/hpa-861-review/`. Its README explicitly calls these textures **candidates**, warns about multi-material junctions, cutout fringes and unverified Godot anchors, and says the previews are not native-render proof. HPA-863 must run native visual validation before accepting an asset. **Before wiring runtime references, move accepted cutouts/atlases (and accompanying .import/UID metadata when applicable) to a live path such as `assets/sprites/world-v2/`.** `export_presets.cfg` excludes `assets/sprites/hpa-861-review/*` from the macOS export; referencing that directory in a production scene would omit the texture from the packaged game. Preserve source sheets/prompt records as provenance in an excluded source/review location, then prune obsolete review scenes, previews and rejected candidates. Verify that the exported package actually includes/loads the accepted textures, not merely that a binary exists. Any genuinely missing/rejected newly generated art goes to a **separate art ticket**; do not mix image generation or SFX production into this implementation.

## Non-negotiable gameplay and architecture boundaries

- Standard Godot 4.7.1, typed GDScript, existing GameSession, WorldShell, FarmMap, TownMap, FarmView and GameMap. **No new renderer, content registry, runtime JSON importer, shader framework, persistence migration or second PR.**
- Keep 1920×1080 UI and the existing 3× world-camera zoom (640×360 world view). Keep the 64×32 projection, nearest filtering, bottom-centre ground contact, ground-plane shadows and Entities as the only Y-sort owner.
- **WorldContract is authoritative** for farm map dimensions, spawn, farm patch, interactable cells/regions, ordered terrain materials, blocking-object footprints/anchors, fence runs and camera bounds. Author *visual-only* decorations in the scene without duplicating unused contract entries. One committed **headless authoring script**, `tools/stamp_farm_ground.gd`, converts contract terrain data to ordinary static Godot TileMapLayer data; no JSON loader, terrain generator or second geometry source runs in game. Keep WorldShell, FarmView and GameSession free of new map constants.
- **TownContract and the town layout do not change.** Only town terrain, backdrop, art frames and optional edge decorations are refreshed.
- Farm E actions remain **bed/door, shipping bin and truck**. The truck arrival is a spawn position, not a fourth E command. The approved layout also marks the 30-cell plot as interactive terrain; that remains **Space-based farming**, not a new E action. Shrine, lots, board, greenhouse, orchard, fallow plots and pier are scenery only.
- Economy, stamina, time, shipping, farming actions, travel duration, NPCs, save **schema** and result tiers remain untouched. **Pre-v2 saves are intentionally rejected**, not respawned: `SessionStateValidator` validates the persisted 30 farm-cell coordinates against `WorldContract.farm_cells()`, which moves with the patch. A new save made in v2 can Continue at the new authored farm spawn because player position and map location are transient. Do not add migration or a save-version compatibility path.

The supplied JSON contains **28 ordered terrain rectangles** painted last-writer-wins. `WorldContract.material_at(cell)` resolves the **final** material (with clamp-to-edge lookup outside the map); only that resolved terrain contributes blocking cells. Do not union raw blocking rectangles: later road strokes reopen `(42..43, 0..2)` through the forest, and the pier at `(23..24, 35)` reopens water — eight cells would be incorrectly blocked. Forest, bamboo, water, reeds and paddy in the **final** grid block movement; scene blockers include building/prop footprints and 85 fence cells. Flower beds, torii, lot ropes and fallow plots remain walk-through. A preliminary 4-neighbour layout-grid audit found **1,129 connected cells before interactable collision**, including the shipping-bin and truck cells; both are occupied by physical colliders, leaving **1,127 nominally walkable cells**. Assert both counts and the specific reopened cells, then check real player clearance and all gates in Godot. For shortest-path timings, use Godot's existing `AStarGrid2D` with contract blockers and weighted projected movement, not a new pathfinding system.

**HPA-743 door interaction trap:** The old bed E region is the intersection of the house footprint with the bed cell. In v2 the 3×3 house ends at y=10 and the door cell begins at y=10, so that intersection becomes `Rect2()` **at the origin**, not a useful contact region. Use the exact narrow southern house slice `Rect2(BED_CELL.x, HOUSE_FOOTPRINT.end.y - 0.25, 1.0, 0.25)`; assert `has_area()` for every `WorldContract.interact_regions()` footprint. Pin physical facing/contact tests at the door. Do not change the 14-world-pixel contact-first E rule, make the whole house interactive, or revive distant raw targets.

## Terrain and backdrop rendering

1. **Authoring is deterministic and committed.** Add a small **mandatory** headless GDScript authoring tool at `tools/stamp_farm_ground.gd`. It reads the 28 ordered material rectangles from `WorldContract.material_at()` and uses **one pure `tile_for(cell)` selection function** shared with a headless smoke test. Re-running the stamp on the same contract must reproduce identical authored `Ground` and `Backdrop` tile placements; the smoke compares those layers to independently recomputed expected cells and pins a few fixed edge/junction fixtures. The tool can use supplied `ground-v2/multi-junctions.json` as an **editor/headless authoring input**; neither this JSON nor any tile generator is a runtime dependency.
2. **Terrain selection.** Pick the simplest accepted HPA-861 64×32 four-phase atlas and supplied precomposed transitions. Use cell phases `(posmod(x, 2), posmod(y, 2))` because the authored backdrop includes negative cells. For `ground-v2`, each cell's material and vertex/corner majority (alphabetical tie-break) determine one opaque atlas tile from the supplied four-phase two-material transitions or multi-junctions. Resolve neighboring materials through `material_at()`; **outside the playable map, clamp each coordinate to the nearest edge cell** rather than hardcoding grass. This continues west/north river, east road/paddy and south water without a visible rim. No real-time blender or new renderer.
3. **One farm painter, no duplicate rules.** The authoring tool stamps all 48×40 playable cells and `WorldMath.backdrop_cells()` overdraw into the authored `Ground` and `Backdrop` TileMapLayers, then saves ordinary Godot scene/TileSet resources. The runtime `FarmMap` no longer calls `GameMap._fill_backdrop()` or carries `_is_backdrop_road/_is_backdrop_water`; retire `BACKDROP_ROAD_ROW` when unused. With opaque ground-v2 tiles, remove empty superseded `Water`/`Paths` layers and old tile-specific smoke assumptions. **TownMap keeps its runtime shared backdrop painter**, retargeted to compatible live grass art; its 16×12 layout and TownContract are unchanged.
4. **Visual coverage.** Review 1920×1080 native captures at zoom 3 for continuous grass, dirt/path/yard junctions, river/pond bank corners, the road/paddy edge and the town's formerly dotted paving. The supply README's four-phase repeats and 26 multi-material tiles are candidates, not accepted proof; reject obvious patterns or fringes rather than silently shipping them.
5. **Camera.** Use `WorldMath.map_camera_bounds(WorldContract.MAP_SIZE)` as the single derivation for the farm bounds: `Rect2(-512, -96, 2816, 1504)`. The existing helper **already includes** `CAMERA_TOP_PADDING`; do not add it twice. Keep zoom 3 and native 1080p UI unchanged.
## Scenery, depth and collisions

- The layout has 60 object records, **not 60 new sprite nodes**: nine records describe ground areas (farmable plot, three fallow plots and five lots), while the bed is a contact region on the house. There are 50 sprite/prop entries including the existing shipping bin and truck, many already present in older form. Author only necessary visual nodes; do not create fake `Entities` children for terrain rectangles.
- Keep a compact `WorldContract` blocking-prop table with `{node, footprint}` records, and **12 straight fence runs** (seven bamboo, five wood) encoding the 35+50 occupied fence cells and approved gaps. Walk-through decor that has no collision or gameplay consumer can be directly positioned in the scene; it needs no duplicate contract record.
- Add a focused `GameMap._add_collisions(body, rects)` helper to create `CollisionPolygon2D` children from contract rectangles via the existing `WorldMath.footprint_to_polygon()`. Derive terrain collision row-runs **from the final last-writer-wins material grid**, not source region unions. Preserve named `HouseCollision`, `ShippingCollision`, and `TruckCollision` for existing tests; derive other collisions rather than hand-authoring ~130 children in the scene. Contract-driven smoke checks assert expected collider and corresponding prop, its anchored position, and its blocked/walk-through intent.
- Props remain under `FarmMap/Entities`, the only Y-sort owner. Position blocking props from `WorldMath.footprint_ground_anchor()`; keep bottom-centre art contact, ground-plane shadows, nearest filtering and `player_occluder` groups only where foreground fade makes sense. Test overlap at large buildings, fences and bin with actual movement.
- Keep 30 crops and soil decals owned by `FarmView`. Use the HPA-861 128×144 crop atlas (4×3, with 32×48 frames) and 128×32 two-frame soil atlas; keep dry/wet/rain state, mature sparkle and GameRules stage mapping unchanged. **`FarmActionEffects` also preloads the old crop art for its harvest pop; switch it to `FarmView.CROP_TEXTURE` so regular crops and harvested-pop sprites always share one texture.**
- Keep the town prop locations, villagers, truck, collision and interactables unchanged while changing the texture frames to the exported live cutouts. Add only approved edge dressing. The shrine, lots, board, greenhouse, orchard, fallow plots and pier have **no additional E actions**.

Test all connected cells and fence gates with the player footprint, plus native `AStarGrid2D` weighted walking from a reachable door-adjacent start to **a legal adjacent/contact cell** of each bin/truck (not into their colliders), ~3–3.5 s at 96 px/s with a documented tolerance.
2. Native-render visual: agent-controlled Godot running at 1920×1080 with zoom 3; compare **front door, field, storehouse yard and town** screenshots side by side against the supplied mockup/blockouts. Verify no grass grid or islands, consistent paths/fences, readable crops and wet soil, seamless town paving and no grey visible at any camera extreme.
3. Input/depth: actual player walks all gate routes, remains visible around every major building/fence and can face/E the three farm interactables from reachable contact; Enter/Space/E and town-return flow continue to work. All scenic objects remain inert on E.
4. Regression: world_shell_smoke, world_math_smoke, GUT, GdUnit4, godot-e2e and existing Promising Farmer/Heart of the Harvest routes pass; tests assert **new** canonical contract, assets, scene nodes and collision coverage rather than retaining hardcoded old frame counts.
5. **Focused MCP-driven end-to-end acceptance:** use a Godot MCP-connected native runtime to drive the **changed map path** through New Game → skip prologue → walk to the new field and perform one Space action → physically approach/E the bin, bed and farm truck → drive into town and return; walk the yard/field gate gaps and try E on one inert prop. Capture front door, field, storehouse yard, town and camera extremes at native 1920×1080. Do **not** replay unrelated harvest-growth, overnight settlement or full Promising/Heart routes through MCP: existing literal GUT/GdUnit4/godot-e2e suites cover those behaviors. Log the MCP actions separately from suite/CI runs; never claim the MCP executed a literal suite it cannot invoke.
6. One platform's agent-driven native validation is sufficient because this changes shared Godot maps, not platform-specific code. **Verify exported macOS package content loads the accepted new textures**; a green export job that only greps for a binary is insufficient. No human approval or human-run test is an acceptance gate.

**Excluded:** lot construction, storage, fallow-plot farming, town re-layout, new art/SFX production, revised gameplay balance, new seasons/systems, screenshots as pixel-golden CI assertions.
