**Status:** Proposed; documentation-only draft PR. No gameplay implementation or verification is claimed here.

**Delivery:** One implementation PR for HPA-863: extend this draft PR, rather than opening another PR. All new art was scoped to HPA-861; HPA-863 only integrates its committed outputs.

## Outcome and visual source of truth

Expand the authored farm from 24×20 to **48×40 logical cells** at the existing 64×32 isometric grid. Preserve the short daily gameplay loop while replacing the raised, visibly repeating terrain and grass-island props with the continuous world shown in the approved Linear mockup.

Treat the following HPA-863 attachments as the approved design inputs, **not runtime data**:
- farm-map-v2-plan.png: numbered 48×40 top-down arrangement.
- farm-map-v2-layout.json: terrain regions, 60 object records, fence positions, footprints, zone names and blocking/decorative intent.
- farm-map-v2-blockout-*.png: expected farm size and front-door, field and storehouse-yard camera compositions.
- world-mockup-vs-game.jpg: target grass, paths, building grounding, soil, crop and HUD composition.

HPA-861 has committed new cutouts, candidate terrain, soil, crops and review scenes under `assets/sprites/hpa-861-review/`. Its README explicitly calls these textures **candidates**, warns about multi-material junctions, cutout fringes and unverified Godot anchors, and says the previews are not native-render proof. HPA-863 must run native visual validation before accepting an asset. **Before wiring runtime references, move accepted cutouts/atlases (and accompanying .import/UID metadata when applicable) to a live path such as `assets/sprites/world-v2/`.** `export_presets.cfg` excludes `assets/sprites/hpa-861-review/*` from the macOS export; referencing that directory in a production scene would omit the texture from the packaged game. Preserve source sheets/prompt records as provenance in an excluded source/review location, then prune obsolete review scenes, previews and rejected candidates. Verify that the exported package actually includes/loads the accepted textures, not merely that a binary exists. Any genuinely missing/rejected newly generated art goes to a **separate art ticket**; do not mix image generation or SFX production into this implementation.

## Non-negotiable gameplay and architecture boundaries

- Standard Godot 4.7.1, typed GDScript, existing GameSession, WorldShell, FarmMap, TownMap, FarmView and GameMap. **No new renderer, content registry, runtime JSON importer, shader framework, persistence migration or second PR.**
- Keep 1920×1080 UI and the existing 3× world-camera zoom (640×360 world view). Keep the 64×32 projection, nearest filtering, bottom-centre ground contact, ground-plane shadows and Entities as the only Y-sort owner.
- **WorldContract is authoritative** for farm map dimensions, spawn, farm patch, interactable cells/regions, ordered terrain materials, blocking-object footprints/anchors, fence runs and camera bounds. Author *visual-only* decorations in the scene without duplicating unused contract entries. One committed **headless authoring script**, `tools/stamp_farm_ground.gd`, converts contract terrain data to ordinary static Godot TileMapLayer data; no JSON loader, terrain generator or second geometry source runs in game. Keep WorldShell, FarmView and GameSession free of new map constants.
- **TownContract and the town layout do not change.** Only town terrain, backdrop, art frames and optional edge decorations are refreshed.
- Farm E actions remain **bed/door, shipping bin and truck**. The truck arrival is a spawn position, not a fourth E command. The approved layout also marks the 30-cell plot as interactive terrain; that remains **Space-based farming**, not a new E action. Shrine, lots, board, greenhouse, orchard, fallow plots and pier are scenery only.
- Economy, stamina, time, shipping, farming actions, travel duration, NPCs, save **schema** and result tiers remain untouched. **Pre-v2 saves are intentionally rejected**, not respawned: `SessionStateValidator` validates the persisted 30 farm-cell coordinates against `WorldContract.farm_cells()`, which moves with the patch. A new save made in v2 can Continue at the new authored farm spawn because player position and map location are transient. Do not add migration or a save-version compatibility path.

The supplied JSON contains **28 ordered terrain rectangles** painted last-writer-wins. `WorldContract.material_at(cell)` resolves the **final** material (with clamp-to-edge lookup outside the map); only that resolved terrain contributes blocking cells. Do not union raw blocking rectangles: later road strokes reopen `(42..43, 0..2)` through the forest, and the pier at `(23..24, 35)` reopens water — eight cells would be incorrectly blocked. Forest, bamboo, water, reeds and paddy in the **final** grid block movement; scene blockers include building/prop footprints and 85 fence cells. Flower beds, torii, lot ropes and fallow plots remain walk-through. A preliminary 4-neighbour layout-grid audit found **1,129 connected cells before interactable collision**, including the shipping-bin and truck cells; both are occupied by physical colliders, leaving **1,127 nominally walkable cells**. Assert both counts and the specific reopened cells, then check real player clearance and all gates in Godot. For reachability, **extend the existing** `tests/headless/world_math_smoke.gd` helpers `_cell_blocked()`, `_flood_reachable()` and `_expect_stand_reachable()` rather than writing a second flood-fill. For path timing, use a tiny **test-local `AStarGrid2D` subclass** overriding `_compute_cost()` and `_estimate_cost()` using `WorldMath.grid_delta_to_world()`; unmodified AStar costs are wrong for 2:1 projection (32 px for one diagonal, 64 px for the other, vs 35.78 px for cardinal steps). Measure from the door-adjacent legal start to the **nearest reachable interaction stance**, not the occupied bin/truck cell: shipping contact candidates `(22,18)` ≈3.28 s / `(23,19)` ≈3.35 s; truck contact `(32,13)` ≈3.24 s / `(32,12)` ≈3.28 s. Forbid diagonal corner cutting and document tolerance; do not add a new navigation subsystem.

**HPA-743 door interaction trap:** The old bed E region is the intersection of the house footprint with the bed cell. In v2 the 3×3 house ends at y=10 and the door cell begins at y=10, so that intersection becomes `Rect2()` **at the origin**, not a useful contact region. Use the exact narrow southern house slice `Rect2(BED_CELL.x, HOUSE_FOOTPRINT.end.y - 0.25, 1.0, 0.25)`; assert `has_area()` for every `WorldContract.interact_regions()` footprint. Pin physical facing/contact tests at the door. Do not change the 14-world-pixel contact-first E rule, make the whole house interactive, or revive distant raw targets.

## Terrain and backdrop rendering

1. **One deterministic source manifest, both TileSet and layers.** Add mandatory `tools/stamp_farm_ground.gd`. From one **ordered** `source_id -> res://assets/sprites/world-v2/ground/...` manifest it generates/updates `scenes/world/terrain_tileset.tres` and all farm `Ground`/`Backdrop` TileMapLayer cell data together. HPA-861 `ground-v2` supplies **11** four-phase material sheets, **23** A-to-B transition sheets (16 corner masks × 4 phases each) and **one** `multi-junctions.png` with 26 indexed entries: **35 atlas sources / ~1,542 tile entries**. Never hand-register ~1,500 entries or let source IDs drift. A pure `tile_for(cell)` under the authoring helper drives stamping and an independent no-drift smoke test. A small phase-only town stamping path may share this same script; **no second general-purpose generator** is introduced.
2. **Authoring source and scene boundaries.** `WorldContract` supplies the **28 ordered terrain rects** and one `material_at(cell)` applying last-writer-wins. For farm overdraw, clamp out-of-bounds material queries to the nearest playable edge (not grass). Use `posmod(x,2)`/`posmod(y,2)` for the four phases, including negative backdrop coordinates. Apply HPA-861 vertex-majority and alphabetic tie-breaking, supplied two-material transitions and multi-junction lookup; each tile is one opaque atlas tile. `tools/stamp_farm_ground.gd` writes ordinary committed resources and `WorldMath.backdrop_cells()` coverage; **the running game never reads layout JSON nor executes terrain selection**.
3. **Town completely decouples before the farm atlas changes.** The current `TownMap/Backdrop` uses `terrain_tileset.tres` plus shared `GameMap._fill_backdrop()` with `WorldContract.BACKDROP_GRASS_TILE`/ROAD_TILE. Those source IDs and single-phase coordinates become invalid when the farm tileset switches. In **Task 1**, give **TownMap Ground and Backdrop their own `town_tileset.tres`** using four-phase `grass-phases`, `paving-phases` and `road-phases` (distinct source IDs), and stamp both layers with per-cell `posmod` phases; retain TownContract's authored road rules and unchanged 16×12 geometry. **Do not apply the farm vertex/multi-junction solver to town**: its `paving/road/grass` border has four three-material junctions missing from `ground-v2/multi-junctions.json`. Make acceptable joins using supplied town tiles and static authoring; if genuinely missing art is necessary, scope a separate art task.
4. **Retire obsolete runtime backdrop coupling.** After both maps have authored backdrops, delete `GameMap._fill_backdrop()`, the old FarmMap road/water backdrop methods and **all four** `WorldContract.BACKDROP_*` constants. Empty superseded `Water`/`Paths` layers may be removed with their obsolete tile tests, because ground-v2 carries its transitions on one opaque tile. Keep the reusable `GameMap` collision helpers.
5. **Native visual checks.** At native 1920×1080 and zoom 3, verify continuous grass, dirt/yard/path joints, water banks, road/paddy edges and town paving, including all farm camera extremes. `WorldMath.map_camera_bounds(MAP_SIZE)` already includes top padding, giving `Rect2(-512, -96, 2816, 1504)`. Candidate PNG repeat checks are *not* Godot-render acceptance.

## Scenery, depth and collisions

- The layout has 60 object records, **not 60 new sprite nodes**: nine records describe ground areas (farmable plot, three fallow plots and five lots), while the bed is a contact region on the house. There are 50 sprite/prop entries including the existing shipping bin and truck, many already present in older form. Author only necessary visual nodes; do not create fake `Entities` children for terrain rectangles.
- Keep a compact `WorldContract` blocking-prop table with `{node, footprint}` records, and **12 straight fence runs** (seven bamboo, five wood) encoding the 35+50 occupied fence cells and approved gaps. Walk-through decor that has no collision or gameplay consumer can be directly positioned in the scene; it needs no duplicate contract record.
- Add a focused `GameMap._add_collisions(body, rects)` helper to create `CollisionPolygon2D` children from contract rectangles via `WorldMath.footprint_to_polygon()`. Derive terrain row-run colliders **from the final last-writer-wins grid**, not source region unions; preserve named `HouseCollision`, `ShippingCollision`, and `TruckCollision`. **In the same atomic geometry commit** author every contract-bearing blocker sprite and all 85 fence visuals, so contract-derived node/collision smokes can actually pass. Do not push a half-placed scene with temporary test exemptions. Task 4 adds only walk-through decor and visual offsets, not required blockers.
- Props remain under `FarmMap/Entities`, the only Y-sort owner. Position blocking props from `WorldMath.footprint_ground_anchor()` using **full-cell physical footprints**; keep bottom-centre art contact, ground-plane shadows, nearest filtering and `player_occluder` groups only where foreground fade makes sense. The full-cell shipping bin changes its old cell-centre visual root and Y-tie/occluder test poses; check contact and pixel anchoring in-engine. The replacement 224×192 farmhouse cutout uses **native scale 1**, retiring the old House root `scale=Vector2(2,2)`. Test overlap at large buildings, fences and bin with actual movement.
- Keep 30 crops and soil decals owned by `FarmView`. Use the HPA-861 128×144 crop atlas (4×3, with 32×48 frames) and 128×32 two-frame soil atlas; keep dry/wet/rain state, mature sparkle and GameRules stage mapping unchanged. **`FarmActionEffects` also preloads the old crop art for its harvest pop; switch it to `FarmView.CROP_TEXTURE` so regular crops and harvested-pop sprites always share one texture.**
- Keep the town prop locations, villagers, truck, collision and interactables unchanged while changing texture frames to the **town-owned four-phase tileset and exported cutouts**. Add only approved edge dressing. The shrine, lots, board, greenhouse, orchard, fallow plots and pier have **no additional E actions**.
- Update `HomesteadAmbience` in the **same geometry cutover**: its current three river ripple anchors depend on old `RIVER_WEST_FOOTPRINT`/`RIVER_SOUTH_FOOTPRINT` and hard-coded positions (6.5/13.0). Derive three visible ripple positions from the new contract's final water cells; the ambience node and effects stay unchanged. Do not leave old river constants referenced after changing the map.
